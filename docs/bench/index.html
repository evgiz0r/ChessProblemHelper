Composing Bench

drag pieces on, around and off the board; transform; solve; every step is written to the journal journal: local only

Board

Pick a piece below and click a square to place it. Click a piece on the board, then its destination, to move it. Del or right-click removes. Transforms move the whole position.

Solve

Set up a position and press Solve.
Pre-key: the diagram is White to move; you get set play, tries with refutations, keys, duals and changed mates.
Post-key: the diagram is the position after the key, Black to move; you get the threat, every Black move with its mates, duals and holes.
