# E. Bourd's published record (what the databases and his mail show)

Compiled 11 Oct 2026 from his PDB export (`knowledge/sources/pdb_bourd_2026-10-11.tex`, query A='Bourd',
246 problems) and a read-only search of his Gmail. The 186 problems not already in Problemesis are the
`pdb_bourd` collection (`python -m chesscomp.db list --source WCCT` etc.; records `pdb-P…`).

## PDB at a glance
- 246 problems, 2001-2018: 181 alone, 43 with Arieh Grinblat, the rest with Witztum, Navon, Zamanov, Retter,
  Avner, Shapiro, Erenburg and others. Genres: h# 97, s# 74, #3 27, #n 24, #2 14, fairy 6, r# 3, retro 1.
- Nothing after 2018 in PDB (one 2018 entry). He told a correspondent in June 2023 that he had stopped
  composing; he resumed in this repository (2026).
- Ours but not in PDB: Variantim 2015 #2635 (3rd Comm., Israel Ring) and Novice Composers TT 2002 (5th place).

What the mail adds (mail before mid-2010 is missing, so the 8th WCCT cannot be checked there):
- 9th: the Bourd/Grinblat candidates were mailed 10 Nov 2011 with his grades; Israel had only their three #3s
  (two best count). He ranked the #4 first, the #6 with 1.Sf6 second. The s#4 was improved on Paz Einat's
  suggestion. A note to himself of 7 Dec 2013, "problem from wcct not sent", holds a truncated position.
  Israel finished 3rd.
- 10th: on 27 Mar 2016 he sent Grinblat the 8 problems given to the captain, 6 joint and 2 alone; PDB has
  5 joint, so one joint candidate was not entered or scored nothing ("Israeli problems for WCCT.pdf",
  mail of 15-17 Apr 2016, settles it). The #5 C040 was improved there to remove a promoted knight. The s#3
  F032 was built with remarks by Rotenberg, Erenburg and the captain, who ruled it "100% yours". He judged
  the #2 candidates for Israel (with Paz Einat) and had no #2 entry. Israel finished 4th.
- 11th (2021): no entry of his; he was on the panel choosing the team's #3 and h# entries.
- 12th (2025-26): no entry; he only received the captain's circulars.

## Other

- FIDE Album 2016-18: he submitted in several sections and judged the moremovers. The Belgrade 2016 WCCC
