## Disagreements and what they teach
1. **"Theme only in a try" is full fulfilment when the theme says "try and/or solution"** (A040 = J57, Bykov/Belchikov, 2nd-3rd,
   our 41st; A039 = J65, white correction on two thematic lines, 15th, our 58th). Our judges wrote "theme only in the capturing king
   try" and "the key is not thematic" and marked down. The countries paid for the whole phase complex: an ideal Hannelius with
   Dombrovskis elements and a Zagoruiko over four phases (Israel: "would score even higher if the 1.Kxa3 phase had been the
   solution"). Read the set theme literally: if it admits tries, a thematic try is not a half-theme.
2. **The 1st place is a logical, multistage fight** (A049 = J71, our 14th): tries first prevent the pinning move, then abandon the
   prevention and unpin the indirectly pinned bishop instead; white correction, three changed mates. Israel: "the highest thematic
   content, combined with clear logic". Our judge saw the correction and the changes (8) but not the logical progression of the
   tries as the content. Score the logic of the try sequence, not only the count of changes.
3. **White units without a function after the key are punished hard** (GBR general remark: "invariably led to a reduction"). A023
   (J66, our 24th, 50th-52nd: "two white bishops that have no post-key function"), A060 (J86, our 45th, last placed: "Rb3 and Be1
   without a function after the key"). Our judges noted these units and kept the scores; the countries cut them. This matches
   E. Bourd's own rule (a unit there only for a try is a drawback): weigh it heavier.
4. **Same scheme twice, both ours high** (A012/A004 = J18/J16, Kxc7 Hannelius, our 21st/34th; real 45th / not published). The
   judge saw the twin and still scored both 6.5-7.5: originality inside the field counts too.
5. **Over-generous on the unpublished ones** (A088 = J31, fourfold Fleck, our 13th, 3.8 points, not published). No comment in the
   report; probably anticipation or the theme judged incidental (the GBR remark: "the set theme appears as an incidental feature ...
   valid only if the work is otherwise interesting and original").
